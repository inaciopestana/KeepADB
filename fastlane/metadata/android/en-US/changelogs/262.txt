Two home-screen widgets: controls (Wireless Debugging, Keep-Alive, webhook sync) and overview (address, Wi-Fi, webhook delivery). Privacy section with three switches; the notification one has a lock-screen option. Tap the eye to hide or show a screen, hold it for the switches. New installs show Wi-Fi names and addresses in notifications until that switch is on. Missed a Wi-Fi prompt? Networks lists it to trust or block. Saving the webhook URL sends one report. Webhook withdrawals are retried.
